# Salt Crown language

**Crown**: The immobile objective whose capture ends a battle.
**Ward**: An immobile explosive defense, defeated only by a Sapper.
**Regent**: The strongest ranked unit, vulnerable to an attacking Shade.
**Shade**: A low-ranked infiltrator whose ambush defeats a Regent.
**Runner**: A unit able to travel any clear distance in a straight line.
**Reveal**: Public knowledge of a unit's identity, retained after combat or a long Runner move.
**Arrangement**: The army's placement before battle.
**Ply**: One faction's completed action.
