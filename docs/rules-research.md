# Official rules research

Reviewed on 2026-10-11 using the publisher-distributed [PlayMonster / Royal Jumbo Classic manual](https://www.playmonster.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/7471_Classic_English_Rules.pdf) and located the [Hasbro manual](https://www.hasbro.com/common/instruct/Stratego.PDF). The PlayMonster text was the directly readable reference; its Classic edition has thirty units, and its optional Duel variant has ten. We did not assume all editions share army size or numbering.

The publisher manual confirms straight orthogonal movement, blocked lakes and occupied squares, immobile bombs/flags, higher-number rank victory, mutual equal-rank removal, stationary winning defenders, Miner bomb removal, attacking Spy exception against Marshal, Scout long-distance movement/attack and revelation, Flag capture, and no-legal-action defeat. It prohibits consecutive back-and-forth oscillation. Our compact game preserves those tactical conventions with original names, reduces the field/army, keeps revealed survivors visible, and replaces oscillation restrictions with exact threefold repetition and eighty quiet actions. Full adaptations and special matchups are in README and the in-game Field guide.

Only rules were researched; no source text, art, symbols or branding was copied into the presentation.
